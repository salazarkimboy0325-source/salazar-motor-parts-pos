Help & Tutorials

Welcome to Motor Parts POS

This Point of Sale system is designed for motor parts shops. Here's a quick overview:

1

Main Inventory Page

Browse and search your inventory. Click the icon to add items to cart.

2

Cart Panel (Right Side)

View items in your cart. Click "Proceed to Checkout" when ready.

3

Checkout Page

Review items, apply discounts, choose payment method, and complete the sale.

4

Management Page

Add, edit, delete products. Manage categories and bulk import items.

5

Reports Page

View sales analytics, track expenses, monitor low stock items.

Navigation Buttons (Top Right):

  • Reports - View analytics and reports
  • Management - Manage inventory and products
  • Settings - System settings
  • Help - This tutorial

Inventory Guide

Searching Products

Use the search bar at the top to find products by:

  • Part Name - e.g., "Brake Pad"
  • Part Number/SKU - e.g., "BRK-001"
  • Barcode - Scan or type barcode
  • Brand - e.g., "NGK", "Brembo"
  • Vehicle - e.g., "Honda Civic"
  • Category - e.g., "brake", "engine"

Category Filter

Use the dropdown below the inventory header to filter by category.

Adding Items to Cart

Click the button on any item to add it to cart.

Note: Out of stock items cannot be added to cart. They show a grayed out button.

Stock Indicators

  • Normal - Item is in stock
  • Low Stock (≤5) - Running low
  • Out of Stock (0) - Needs restock

Cart & Checkout Guide

Managing Cart

  • View Cart: Right panel shows all items with quantities
  • Remove Item: Click the button
  • Clear Cart: Click "Clear Cart" button at bottom

Checkout Process

  1. Click "Proceed to Checkout"
  2. Enter customer name (optional)
  3. Enter vehicle info (optional)
  4. Apply discount if needed (% or amount)
  5. Choose payment method
  6. Enter payment details
  7. Click "Process Payment"
  8. Receipt will be displayed

Payment Methods

  • Cash: Enter amount received, system calculates change
  • Card: Enter card details
  • GCash: Enter reference number
  • Bank Transfer: Select bank and enter reference

Management Guide

Adding a New Part

  1. Click "Add New Part"
  2. Fill in required fields (*):
    • Part Number/SKU
    • Part Name
    • Unit Price
  3. Optional fields: Category, Brand, Vehicles, Cost, Stock, Supplier
  4. Click "Save Part"

Bulk Import

Add multiple parts at once:

  1. Click "Bulk Add"
  2. Enter one part per line in format:
    3. PartNumber, Name, Category, Price, Cost, Stock, Brand, Supplier
  3. Example: BRK-001, Brake Pad Set, brake, 45.00, 30.00, 10, Brembo, AutoSupply

Managing Categories

  • Click "Categories" button
  • Add new categories
  • Rename existing categories
  • Delete unused categories

Reports Guide

Available Reports

  • Summary Cards: Total sales, revenue, customers, items sold
  • Sales Chart: Daily, weekly, or monthly sales trends
  • Top Selling Items: Best performing products
  • Low Stock Alert: Items needing restock
  • Sales by Category: Revenue breakdown
  • Payment Methods: How customers pay
  • Recent Transactions: All sales in date range
  • Expense Tracker: Track business expenses
  • Inventory Valuation: Total stock value
  • Top Customers: Best customers
  • Hourly Sales: Peak hours

Date Range

Use the date buttons or custom date picker to filter all reports by time period.

Tips & Tricks

  • Quick Search: Type in search bar and press Enter to add matching item to cart
  • Quick Cash: Click preset amounts (₱50, ₱100, etc.) for fast cash payment
  • Data Persistence: All data is saved automatically in your browser
  • Export: Download transactions and low stock lists as CSV files
